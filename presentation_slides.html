  • Production Architecture: Flutter 3.x (Riverpod 2.x) frontend paired with NestJS 10.x (PostgreSQL 16 + Prisma ORM) backend services.
  • Quality Assurance: Verified 100% test pass rate across unit and widget test suites (0 errors).

🌾 Operational Capabilities

  • Live Revenue Metrics: Real-time tracking of total gross merchandise value (GMV) and active inventory count.
  • Direct Inventory Listing: 1-tap produce listing with pricing per unit/kg and instant stock level toggles.
  • Sequential Pipeline: Transition incoming orders step-by-step (`1. Confirm` ➔ `2. Pack` ➔ `3. Ready for Pickup`).
  • Reason-Based Order Rejection: Order rejection with dropdown reasons (*Harvest Shortfall*, *Weather Damage*) and clear warning banners.
